[h1]Zellno Prison Bear[/h1]

Zellno Prison Bear is a small config-only modification for DayZ that provides an independent, resilient variant of the vanilla brown bear.

It was designed for controlled encounters, particularly around the Black Market area on Prison Island in Chernarus.

[h2]Classname[/h2]
[code]Animal_UrsusArctos_ZellnoPrison[/code]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Independent classname derived from the vanilla brown bear.
[*]Does not modify vanilla bears globally.
[*]Vanilla model, textures, animations, sounds and AI.
[*]Vanilla movement, attacks, skinning resources and yields.
[*]Global Health: 15,000.
[*]Global Blood: 50,000.
[*]Global Shock: 5,000.
[*]No custom or third-party assets.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*]DayZ.
[*]The mod must be loaded by both the server and connecting clients.
[/list]

PvZmoD Spawn System is not a dependency. The classname can be spawned with VPPAdminTools, mission scripts or compatible event and spawn systems.

Static-spawn coordinates are intentionally not included because navigation, containment and balance depend on each server's environment.
