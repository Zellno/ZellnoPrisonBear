# Architecture

`Animal_UrsusArctos_ZellnoPrison` inherits directly from the official
`Animal_UrsusArctos` classname.

Only `DamageSystem/GlobalHealth` is declared by the custom classname.
The vanilla bear classname and its AI template are not modified.

Inherited features include the model, textures, sounds, animations,
simulation, movement, attacks, damage output and skinning configuration.

The project contains no copied DayZ assets and no content from third-party
mods.
